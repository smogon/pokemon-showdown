Translation notes
=================

See [../README.md] for important notes for translators.

Unlike the games, we use `.` instead of `,` as the decimal separator. Both are allowed, RAE prefers `.`, and even though the official Spanish translation is from Spain, most fans are from LatAm and prefer `.`.

Mistakes in official text
-------------------------

The translations here are, where possible, sourced verbatim from the official games. Here are likely mistakes in official game translations, in case you choose to change them.

| | Text |
|---|---|
| **Official** | `¡La habilidad Paleosíntesis de {POKEMON} se ha activado gracial al sol!` |
| **Suggested** | `¡La habilidad Paleosíntesis de {POKEMON} se ha activado gracias al sol!` |
| **Official** | `¡{POKEMON} ahora va a a por todas!` |
| **Suggested** | `¡{POKEMON} ahora va a por todas!` |
